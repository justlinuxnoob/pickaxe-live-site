💣 Falling Pickaxe LIVE

A live game on YouTube: one giant pickaxe falls forever through a world of blocks, and the chat controls it.

How to play

  1. Open the Falling Pickaxe LIVE stream on YouTube.
  2. Type a word in the live chat. Your name pops up on screen right away.
  • tnt — drops a TNT with your name next to the pickaxe
  • fast / slow — the pickaxe falls faster or slower for a while
  • big — the pickaxe grows huge for a while
  • wood, stone, iron, gold, diamond, obsidian — switch the pickaxe material

❤️ Every like drops a TNT, and every 25 likes start a pickaxe rain. ⭐ A new subscriber drops a MEGA TNT. When the pickaxe reaches the bottom, the world is complete and a new one starts.
