Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Falling Pickaxe LIVE is a game shown on its own YouTube channel. This policy explains what the game uses and why.

What the game reads

  • Public live-chat messages on our own live streams, with the sender's public YouTube display name and channel ID. This is how chat words like "tnt" work and how your name appears on screen.
  • Public counts of our own live streams and channel: likes, concurrent viewers and subscriber count, used for the like rewards and on-stream thank-yous.
  • Super Chats, memberships and gifts sent to our channel (name and amount), so supporters can be thanked on stream.
  • Subscribers whose subscriptions are public, so they can be thanked by name on stream.

What is stored

Leaderboards (display name and number of TNTs dropped) and world records are kept on our game server so they survive restarts. Chat messages are not stored, and only recognised game words are shown on screen. We do not collect email addresses, passwords or payment details.

Google / YouTube access

The game signs in only to the channel owner's own YouTube account (scope youtube.force-ssl). It uses that access to read the live chat and the statistics listed above, and to manage the channel's own live broadcasts and titles. It does not access any viewer's account. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. YouTube's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data. Display names appear on the public live stream as part of the game.

Moderation and removal

Names pass a profanity filter. To have your name removed from the leaderboards, contact us through the YouTube channel's About page.
